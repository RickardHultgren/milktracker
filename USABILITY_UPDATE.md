# Main-screen usability update

The main screen was simplified for one-handed use while collecting milk.

## Changes

- Current amount owed is the largest information element at the top.
- Half-can and full-can are the two dominant actions.
- Each quick action is 128 logical pixels high.
- The quantity is the largest text on each action button.
- Cost is shown immediately below the quantity.
- "Other amount" is visually secondary.
- History and Settings remain small app-bar actions.
- The payment action becomes prominent only when the threshold has been reached.
- No changes were made to ledger, storage, payment, or accounting behavior.
